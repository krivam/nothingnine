Tell me when it launches What is inside

The day, read the traditional way

The five angas of the panchangam for any date, plus the timings and festivals people actually check.

Tithi and nakshatram

The running tithi and nakshatram with the exact time each one ends, so you know when the day turns.

Yogam, karanam, vaaram

All five angas together on one screen, in Telugu, without scrolling through a printed almanac.

Sunrise and sunset

Local sunrise and sunset for the day, the basis for every other timing shown in the app.

Auspicious and avoid windows

The good and bad periods of the day marked clearly, in the order you would read them.

Festival calendar

Telugu months and festivals across the year, browsable by month and by date.

Works with no internet

Everything is computed on the device. No sign-in, no sync, nothing to download after install.

For reference only

Timings are computed for your location and may differ slightly from a printed panchangam followed by your family or temple.

Privacy and support

No ads, no advertising ID, no permissions. Only anonymised analytics and crash reports leave the phone.
