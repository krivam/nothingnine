Privacy Policy for Poddu Telugu Panchangam (పొద్దు తెలుగు పంచాంగం)

Effective Date: September 4, 2026

Poddu Telugu Panchangam ("the App") is developed by NothingNine Apps and is committed to protecting user privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when using the App.

1. Data Processing and Usage

The App works entirely offline. It has no accounts and no sign-in, and never asks for your name, email, phone number or location. The only information that leaves your phone is anonymised analytics and crash reports, described below.

2. Third-Party Services

The App uses two Google services, and no others. There is no advertising in the App.

  • Google Analytics for Firebase receives anonymised app usage, along with a randomly generated app instance ID, your device model, OS version, app version and the country the request arrives from.
  • Firebase Crashlytics receives, only when the App crashes, the technical details of that crash and a randomly generated installation ID that lasts until the App is uninstalled.

All data collected by these SDKs is encrypted in transit using industry-standard protocols (HTTPS/TLS).

Each service processes data according to its own privacy policy:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics

3. How Information Is Used

Information collected by these services is used strictly to:

  • Understand how the App is used, so it can be improved.
  • Detect and fix crashes and technical issues.

NothingNine Apps does not sell, rent, or directly collect any personal user data.

4. No Advertising and No Advertising ID

The App shows no advertisements. The Advertising ID permission that normally arrives with the analytics SDK is removed from the App, and the SDK is told not to look for that identifier, so no Advertising ID is read or sent.

5. Data Deletion and Retention

Since the App does not use accounts or cloud storage, your local data is deleted simply by uninstalling the App or clearing the App's storage in your device settings. Uninstalling also ends the randomly generated IDs described above. Technical data already sent to Google is retained according to their policies.

You may request deletion of anonymised analytics data by contacting us with your App Instance ID. However, because we store no personal identifiers, uninstalling the App is the most effective way to stop and remove this data.

6. User Choices & Consent

  • Opt-out: you may stop all data collection by uninstalling the App.
  • Google-level controls: your Android privacy settings and Google account activity controls also apply to the data these services collect.

7. Permissions

The App requests no permission from you, and asks for nothing sensitive — no contacts, no microphone, no camera, no location, no storage. Internet access is added automatically by the analytics and crash reporting SDKs and is used solely by them; the App itself downloads no content.

8. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. The "Effective Date" at the top will be updated accordingly.

10. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this policy, please contact us at: hi@nothingnine.com
