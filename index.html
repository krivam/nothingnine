VK Financial Calculator

A simple financial utility to calculate and estimate your SIP investment returns, EMI and loan payments, retirement income, chit funds, GST, inflation, and more.

I built it to keep the calculations I reach for in one straightforward Android app—without accounts, sign-ups, or unnecessary setup.

For calculations only. VK Financial Calculator does not provide or promote financial services, products, or advice.

What you can calculate

The app currently brings together calculators across these nine categories.

Mutual Funds

  • Systematic Investment Plan (SIP)
  • SIP Goal (Reverse SIP)
  • Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP)
  • Index Returns (Historic)

Loans

  • Home Loan
  • Home Loan (Advanced)
  • Bike Loan
  • Car Loan
  • Personal Loan

Retirement

  • National Pension System (NPS)
  • Public Provident Fund (PPF)
  • Employee Provident Fund (EPF)
  • Atal Pension Yojana (APY)
  • PM Shram Yogi Maandhan (PMSYM)

Bank Schemes

  • FD Cumulative
  • FD Payout
  • Recurring Deposit (RD)
  • Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)
  • Senior Citizen Savings Scheme (SCSS)
  • Kisan Vikas Patra (KVP)
  • Mahila Samman Savings Certificate (MSSC)

Post Office

  • Post Office Monthly Income (POMIS)
  • Post Office RD (PORD)
  • Post Office Time Deposit (POTD)
  • National Savings Certificate (NSC)

Bonds

  • Sovereign Gold Bond (SGB)

Tax

  • GST Calculator

Employees

  • HRA Exemption
  • Gratuity Calculator

General

  • Future Value
  • Simple Interest
  • Inflation Calculator
  • Inflation (Historic)
  • Discount & Markup Calculator
  • Chit Fund Calculator
  • Jewellery Cost Estimator
